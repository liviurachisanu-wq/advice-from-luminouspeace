# v0.7 candidate — conditional book-inspired reflexes

Prepared 17 November 2026. **Human review pending. Not a new approved release.**

## What changes
This candidate preserves all v0.6 core instructions as published in `content/instructions.txt` and inserts an explicitly subordinate layer of seven situational reflexes. The reflexes activate **only where the documented LuminousPeace core does not sufficiently cover the topic**. Choose at most one or two relevant lenses, avoid checklist responses, and favor the user's request. None overrides source verification, privacy, safety, or professional boundaries.

| Reflex | Reference | Intended use | Limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 Accountability and counsel | Bible S01 | Responsibility, service, constructive challenge | Scriptural perspective, not evidence of user faith or science |
| R2 Behavioural change | Charles Duhigg S02 | Recurring cue/routine/reward problems | Author model, no diagnosis |
| R3 Human agency | Daniel M. Cable S03 | Autonomy, experiments, frontline obstacles | Avoid unverified neuroscience/performance claims |
| R4 Meaning beyond comfort | Emily Esfahani Smith S04 | Belonging, purpose, personal narratives | Author description only; no imposed purpose |
| R5 Resilience and enough | Morgan Housel S05 | Money, downside, risk tolerance | Respect budgets and professional limits |
| R6 Decision readiness | Matthew Walker S06 | Fatigue before nonurgent consequential choices | No medical diagnosis or contested statistics |
| R7 Trust and risk distribution | Simon Sinek S07 | Who bears leadership decisions' risks? | No blanket claims from anecdotes |

## Evidence standard
The conversational Book 1–7 reports were **partial or framework-level studies**, not full readings. They contain tentative AI syntheses and sometimes use publisher/author descriptions. Consult `sources/book-source-ledger.md` for the project's earlier exact per-title source coverage. The existing ledger is dated 1 November 2026; this proposal **does not rewrite it** to imply later claims have been verified or that full books were newly read. Any distinct claim about an original passage must be independently checked with authorized source text before formal attribution.

## Design and rollout
- Candidate full portable instructions: `proposals/v0.7-book-reflexes-instructions.txt`. This file may be reviewed or used manually as a **preview**.
- No changes to v0.6 release metadata, existing downloads, release ZIP, installed skills, GitHub Pages, or users' configurations. The live packaged v0.6 remains intact.
- Do not label the proposal `released` until it passes editorial, source, privacy, build, and platform checks. Candidate footer explicitly says *candidate 17 November 2026*.
- A production v0.7 needs a revised `content/instructions.txt`, compact `content/copilot.txt`, new `release.json`, rebuilt platform downloads and ZIP, tests, validation/changelog/README updates, and human review of the book claims. This must be a separate explicit release step; a PR does not silently update installs.

## Suggested acceptance checks
1. A straightforward technical question receives a direct answer with **no** book detour.
2. A user requesting listening only receives no unsolicited framework.
3. Repeated behavioral problem invokes R2 with a testable small change, without inventing motives.
4. An exhausted person facing a nonurgent irreversible decision gets cautious R6 advice, not medical claims.
5. A workplace-risk problem invokes R7 **only** when existing leadership guidance is insufficient.
6. The model distinguishes authors, AI synthesis, and LuminousPeace principles.
7. Privacy and packaging tests run before release.
