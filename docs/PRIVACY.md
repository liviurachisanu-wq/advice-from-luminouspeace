# Privacy and boundaries

The creator uses the public pseudonym LuminousPeace. The public distribution must exclude private coaching reports, clinical assessments, relationship notes, personal contact information and private-chat links. Content approval is separate from privacy clearance. An audit found disclosures in earlier test fixtures and commit metadata; current-file corrections alone do not remove publicly accessible history. Attribution describes influence; it does not establish personal authorship, a professional relationship or approval of a response.

Downloading these files sends no conversation content to a project-operated service. Pasting them into an AI product is subject to that provider's data handling, account settings and organization rules. This project does not control those systems and cannot promise anonymity or confidential treatment by them.

Do not submit identifiable personal situations in GitHub issues. Redact names, employers, client details and sensitive facts. A public pseudonym does not guarantee anonymity: distinctive language and public references may be recognizable.

The skill helps organize decisions and appropriate professional support. It does not diagnose, certify professionals, or grant permission for external actions. Check professional scope, current credentials, fit and conflicts. Instructions cannot override platform safeguards or account permissions.

The repository links to source material; it does not redistribute complete copyrighted books. Source descriptions, excerpts and unverified identifications must stay clearly distinguished.

Never encode protected names, assessment labels or sensitive facts in public test fixtures or denylists. Automated checks inspect text and bounded ZIP contents for common contact, credential and private-path patterns. They cannot determine whether a narrative identifies someone. Human review must cover names, context, Git commit authors/committers, branches, tags, release assets and hosting logs. Publish with a reviewed no-reply commit identity; replacing a file does not remove older commits. Cleaning public history cannot recall downloaded copies or guarantee removal from caches.
